FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is this website the official ExpressVPN website?

No. This is an independent informational resource that is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by ExpressVPN.

How do I log in to ExpressVPN?

You can log in using your registered email and password on the ExpressVPN app or website. If you have 2FA enabled, you'll need a verification code.

What should I do if I forgot my password?

Use the “Forgot password?” link on the login page to reset it via email. Follow the instructions sent to your registered address.

Can I log in on multiple devices at the same time?

Yes, ExpressVPN allows multiple simultaneous connections depending on your subscription plan. You can log in on all supported devices.
